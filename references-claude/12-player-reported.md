# Player-reported experience after 4.5 (Steam discussions)

Label: **PLAYER-REPORTED**. Read from 4.5-era threads in the [Stellaris Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/), 22 Sep – 19 Oct 2026. These are individual players' experiences, often contradictory. The Guide can phrase them as "players report…" tips, never as rules. The Paradox forum and Reddit were not read (they block automated access).

## Economy and jobs

- **Check job priorities after loading a save and after upgrading a capital building.** One player reports that not every job priority (especially jobs set to 0) survives a reload, and capital upgrades add jobs that pull pops off needed resources. [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/571543229359488641/) (beta, Jul 2026)
- Pops may **avoid jobs for a resource you already have a big surplus of** (example: after a Matter Decompressor, pops stopped taking mining jobs). If the player stockpiles on purpose, they may need to set job priorities. [same thread]
- **Build only when you have unemployed pops to fill the jobs;** early colonies can set Enforcer jobs to 0; don't colonize every planet at once (since 4.3). [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/592939955001524178/) (Aug 2026)
- Some players find the 4.5 economy "feast or famine" and hard to predict. [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/569300630414397344/) (19 Oct 2026). **Guide implication:** runway warnings and small buffers matter more than monthly net.

## Military

- If auto designs keep producing point-defense sections, **change the Fleet Doctrine**; "Screen" roles pick PD sections. Some players say PD/flak corvettes are fine early (strong vs Void Worms and missile openers), others prefer lasers + flak. [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/569300439979295546/) (16 Oct 2026)
- Frigates: players describe them as good against crises, wildlife and stations, weak against most fleets in open space. [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/589565769440910248/) (7 Oct 2026)
- Old starbases may keep low-tier modules; replacing a module makes it update. [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/592940620292846514/) (beta, Sep 2026). 4.5.0 made modules auto-upgrade; this may be older-save behavior.

## AI and stability

- AI is reported as **better in fleet tactics but still weak strategically**; some machine AIs still fall into energy debt. [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/589564502875463149/) (24 Sep 2026). **Guide implication:** don't assume AI neighbors will manage their economy well; a struggling AI may be a target or a weak ally.
- At 4.5.0 launch, players reported **Nomad stockpile collection and fleet automation broken** (fixed in 4.5.1) and multiplayer desyncs. [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/563668523042451952/), [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/563668523042459563/) (22 Sep 2026)
- Late-game lag is still reported by some players on large galaxies. [thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/569300204759752830/) (Oct 2026). **Guide implication:** autosave intervals may stretch in late game; don't assume monthly snapshots.

## Using player reports in the Guide

1. Show them only as optional tips under a recommendation, with "players report".
2. Prefer reports that match the official notes or game files.
3. Re-collect them after each patch; most of these concern 4.5.0–4.5.2 only.
