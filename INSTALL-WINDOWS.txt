STELLARIS GUIDE 1.6.4 — WINDOWS

1. Right-click StellarisGuide-Windows-1.6.4.zip and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder. Double-click StellarisGuide.exe. No mod, Node,
   terminal, or AI account is needed. Check the badge says v1.6.4.
3. Open Stellaris 4.5.2 Cygnus and load your campaign. The Guide reads the
   newest save in your chosen save folder every 15 seconds. Click the round
   refresh button to check now. It never changes your save.
4. If the wrong campaign appears, open Settings and choose its save folder.
   The usual location is Documents\Paradox Interactive\Stellaris\save games.
   Save your desired campaign in Stellaris, then refresh the Guide.

START OFFLINE
The built-in guide, next moves, planets, empire view, technology choices and
ship advice work without the internet. Main shows a pinned campaign goal and
any serious resource warning. Expand a goal to see how to make progress.

TECHNOLOGY ROUTE
On Tech Tree, choose a target and click Explore route. A separate Microsoft
Edge window shows completed, current and possible prerequisites. Its Close
button closes that route window. If Edge is missing, the main Guide still
works, but Explore route cannot open. A route marked Limited means the game
version, DLC, mods, or some rules could not be verified; check Stellaris before
spending research. This release is checked against 4.5.2 Cygnus; later game
versions need a new compatibility check.

OPTIONAL AI AND CAMERA
In Settings you may choose Ollama, LM Studio, or OpenRouter. These are optional.
For screenshots, choose an image-capable model. The camera first tries the
Stellaris window; if unavailable, choose another window or the whole screen.
Select the region, then press Send. OpenRouter receives your question, a
relevant summary from the selected save, and any attached image online.
Local models keep them on your PC. If a model fails, text
questions use the built-in guide; screenshot questions say the image was not
analyzed. Check AI suggestions in game.

HIDDEN INFORMATION
Settings has Show hidden save information (spoilers). It starts off and needs
two confirmations. It turns off again when the selected save changes,
including the next autosave. This prevents a new campaign from inheriting
permission. While off, rival details the player cannot verify stay Unknown.

REOPEN OR QUIT
Closing the browser leaves the Guide running. Right-click its icon near the
Windows clock (click ^ if hidden) to reopen, refresh, or quit. You can also
open Settings and click Quit Stellaris Guide. An older release may run beside
v1.6.4; quit the older icon when you no longer need it.
