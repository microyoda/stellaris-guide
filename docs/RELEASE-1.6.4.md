# Stellaris Guide 1.6.4

This update improves answers, research planning and fleet decisions for Stellaris 4.5 Cygnus campaigns.

## What changed

- **Chat:** Named planet and empire questions use the selected save. The built-in guide separates what the save shows from general rules, asks for a precise name when several places match, and says when the evidence is missing. Optional AI answers receive a smaller, relevant evidence packet and additional unsupported-claim checks.
- **Tech Tree:** A technology card explains what the installed game description says where available and shows up to five possible downstream technologies. Selecting a future card opens its own exact prerequisite route. Uncertain draw, DLC, version and mod conditions remain visible.
- **Fleet Design Finder:** Choose Corvette, Frigate or Destroyer, a goal, and optional enemy defenses or weapons you have observed. The Guide compares your owned saved designs, distinguishes automatic designs and conditional Guide concepts, and explains trade-offs. The candidate list is visible; detailed parts comparison opens on demand.

The Fleet Finder does not know exact build cost, fitting validity, reactor power or combat outcome. It never calls a saved design verified buildable. Check the final parts, power, cost and policy effects in Stellaris Ship Designer before building. Enemy equipment must be entered by the player; hidden enemy designs are not used when spoilers are off. Low-cost, mobility and screening goals remain open questions until the required measurements can be verified.

## Install or update

Extract `StellarisGuide-Windows-1.6.4.zip` into a new folder and double-click `StellarisGuide.exe`. The badge under **STELLARIS GUIDE** should say **v1.6.4**. The older release can stay running during evaluation; use its own tray icon to quit it when ready. See [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) for the full nontechnical guide.

## Compatibility

The guide has been checked against Stellaris 4.5.2 Cygnus save and installed rule evidence. A later game version, different checksum, DLC combination or rules-changing mod can limit a recommendation. The Guide reads saves and does not change the game.
